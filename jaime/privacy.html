Privacy Policy — Jaime Personal Agent

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Who we are

Jaime Personal Agent ("Jaime") is a private personal assistant operated by Vinícius Famim (vfamim@gmail.com) for his own exclusive use. It is not available to the public.

Data accessed

With the owner's explicit OAuth consent, Jaime may access the owner's own Google data: Gmail messages, Google Calendar events, Google Drive files, Google Docs, Google Sheets and Google Tasks, plus basic profile information (name and email address).

How data is used

  • Google data is used only to carry out requests made by the owner, such as summarizing email, checking the calendar, creating tasks or reading documents.
  • Actions that change data, such as sending email, creating or deleting events, or sharing files, are performed only after the owner's explicit confirmation.
  • Google user data is not used for advertising and is not used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, rented or shared with third parties. Request content may be processed by the AI model provider that powers the assistant, only to generate a response for the owner.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials and any retained data are stored on a private server controlled by the owner, with restricted file permissions. Data is kept only as long as needed for the owner's personal use.

Limited Use

Jaime's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
