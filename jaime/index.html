Jaime Personal Agent

Jaime is a private, single-user personal assistant run by Vinícius Famim for his own use. It helps him manage his own calendar, email, tasks and files through Telegram.

The app is not offered to the public and only accesses the Google account of its owner, with his explicit authorization.

  • Google services used: Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets and Google Tasks.
