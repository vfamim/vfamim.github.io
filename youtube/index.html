viostack youtube

viostack youtube is a private, single-user tool run by Vinícius Famim to upload his own videos to his own YouTube channel.

The app is not offered to the public. It only acts on the Google account of its owner, with his explicit authorization.

  • Google service used: YouTube Data API v3, upload permission only (youtube.upload).
